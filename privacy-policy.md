Quem Sou Eu? stores user-generated information locally on the user's device.

If you wish to delete the information stored by Quem Sou Eu?, you can:

* Use any data-clearing or deletion functionality provided within the App, if available;
* Clear the App's storage/data through your Android device settings; or
* Uninstall Quem Sou Eu? from your device.

These actions remove locally stored App data associated with the installation.

Please note that information processed independently by third-party services, such as Google AdMob, is subject to the respective provider's privacy policies and available privacy controls.

If you have questions about information processed by a third-party service, you should contact the relevant service provider directly.
